## Authored game

Game logic, story dialogue, authored editor tools, shaders, procedural geometry,
procedural textures, synthesized audio and the Three.js prototype are MIT licensed
under LICENSE. Models and textures are generated by the included Blender and
Unity scripts. The in-game old photograph is rendered from this game's own
characters by `StoryPhotoSetup.cs`; it is not a photograph of real people.

The project takes general inspiration from small-planet adventures and the story
motif of Oscar Wilde's The Selfish Giant. No third-party translation, reference
game code, models, textures, recordings or tutorial images are included. This
review is based on supplied source and generation records, not a forensic proof
of authorship or a worldwide clearance of every possible similarity.

Unity-derived project configuration originate from the URP blank template
17.0.14 and retain the Unity Companion License. These are not relicensed under
MIT. See LICENSES/Unity-template-notice.md, Unity-template-third-party.md and

include the packages themselves. Package Manager downloads Unity/third-party
packages under their respective licenses. Do not assume they are MIT.
