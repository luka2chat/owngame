# Android artwork and licenses

The interface retains owngame's existing multicolor wordmark, paper background,
outlined controls and arcade mascot. Authored HTML/CSS/SVG and commissioned
owngame illustrations are distributed under the repository MIT license except
where the per-game notices below specify otherwise. The mascot and several
original game illustrations were created with OpenAI ImageGen for owngame.
No private reference photograph, prompt history or device capture is included.

- `assets/art/arcade-mascot.png` and `res/drawable-nodpi/arcade_mascot.png`:
  existing owngame arcade mascot; blue cabinet, cream face and red crown cap.
- Signal Harbor backgrounds, lighthouse and signal sprites: original owngame
  game assets, also available in `examples/signal-harbor`.
- Star Triad Moonlight Enamel and Firefly Four paper assets: existing original
  game backgrounds, boards and pieces from `examples/star-triad` and
  `examples/firefly-four`.
- `assets/art/rollout/`: covers composed from the corresponding playable game's
  actual scene. Their names correspond to the public `examples/` source folders.
  They are not evidence of a human playtest or acceptance of the game's art.
- `rollout/rc-rally.png`: Raycast Vehicle scene/models, MIT, Ian Curtis;
  see the bundled MIT notice and `examples/rc-rally`.
- `rollout/faune-dungeon.png`: Dungeon Tileset II by 0x72 and Legend of Faune
  by ansimuz, CC0; see the bundled CC0 text and `examples/faune-dungeon`.
- `rollout/civ-clicker.png`: CivClicker-derived cover, retained under
  GPL-3.0-or-later. Katherine Stark, Scott A. Colcord and Luke Nickerson;
  icons by Lorc and game-icons.net contributors, CC BY 3.0, adapted by
  Katherine Stark. The owngame village illustration and resource icons were
  commissioned with ImageGen. Full editable game source and notices are in
  `examples/civ-clicker`; this cover is not relicensed as MIT.

Public PNG copies omit non-rendering text, EXIF and embedded provenance metadata.
The pixel data is retained. Original production files are not uploaded.

Full notices and license texts are bundled in `assets/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
and `assets/LICENSES/` so they also travel with a locally built APK. Android SDK,
JDK and external games are installed/downloaded separately under their own terms.

The Android library also bundles the existing website covers for Star Letters
(`star-letters.jpg`), Crowd Runner and Arrow Escape. They are the same game-cover
images already published under `apps/web/public/art/rollout`, from the authored
games in `examples/star-letters`, `examples/crowd-runner` and `examples/arrow-escape`.
They are reused unchanged in appearance; no private photo or new artwork is added.
